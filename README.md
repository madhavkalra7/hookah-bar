# Hookah Baar 😮‍💨

### [hookah.nycanshu.dev](https://hookah.nycanshu.dev)

Smoke a hookah with your hands, in your browser.

Pick up the pipe with your fist, bring it to your lips, breathe in. Take it
away and blow. Make an O for challe, puff your cheeks for a big one.

Six hookahs. Sixteen flavours. Mix any two and share the link.
No coal, no tobacco, no sutta. Just vibes.
